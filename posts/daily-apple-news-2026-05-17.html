💡 Quick Tips

Tip #1: Customize Your CarPlay Layout in iOS 26

With iOS 26's new CarPlay customization options, you can now rearrange and personalize your CarPlay home screen. Go to Settings → General → CarPlay, select your vehicle, and tap "Customize." You can drag apps into your preferred order, hide apps you don't use, and configure widget layouts — all from your iPhone without touching the car's display.

Tip #2: Check Your MacBook's Battery Health Before Buying a New One

Before taking advantage of the great MacBook deals this month, check your current MacBook's battery health. Click the Apple menu → System Settings → Battery → Battery Health to see your maximum capacity. If it's above 80%, your battery is still in good shape and you might be fine waiting for the M6 MacBook Pro redesign later this year.

Tip #3: Lock Your Mac Screen When Your Cat Walks Across the Keyboard

A fun new indie Mac app called Cats Lock solves the age-old problem of cats triggering keyboard inputs when they walk across your MacBook. The app detects unusual keyboard patterns and automatically locks the screen, preventing accidental emails, deleted files, and other feline chaos. Search for "Cats Lock" on the Mac App Store — it's a lifesaver for cat-owning Mac users.
